# Task Plot Brief

## Task

- Title: Passive Lottery Task
- Construct: expectancy processing / outcome processing / value-valence processing
- Paradigm: passive observation of gain, loss, and mixed lotteries with sampled outcomes.

## Rows

- Gain lottery: 75% chance +10, otherwise 0.
- Loss lottery: 75% chance -10, otherwise 0.
- Mixed lottery: 75% chance +10, otherwise -10.

## Trial Timeline

1. Condition cue: 600 ms. Show condition label, no response.
2. Pre-lottery fixation: 1200 ms fixation, no response.
3. Lottery reveal: 1500 ms. Show probability and two possible outcomes, no response.
4. Outcome feedback: 1000 ms. Show realized outcome and cumulative score, no response.
5. ITI: 800 ms fixation, no response.

## Notes

- No trial-level participant response is required.
- Outcome is pre-sampled from each profile and then shown on the feedback screen.
